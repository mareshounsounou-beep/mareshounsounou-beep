## 📧 LinkedIn Posts

### Post 1 (Authority)
```
200+ placements. 95% retention at 12 months. Here's what I've learned about hiring great engineers.

1. **Stop trying to find a unicorn**
A unicorn doesn't exist. Find someone with potential + hunger to learn.

2. **Technical skills are table stakes**
Culture fit + growth mindset matters more.

3. **Speed is competitive advantage**
Best talent has options. 2-3 week process or they're gone.

4. **Compensation matters, but so does growth**
50% salary, 30% growth, 20% opportunity.

5. **Onboarding is day 1 retention**
The first 3 months determine if they stay 5+ years.

Hiring is hard. But these fundamentals help.

What else would you add?

#Hiring #HRTech #Recruitment
```
